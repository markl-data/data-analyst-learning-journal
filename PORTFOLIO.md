## Featured Project

### Financial Statements Analysis (2009-2022)

A three-question analytical investigation of 12 major listed companies across 14 years, delivering ranked performance findings, sector-level insights, and structural observations on the mega-cap divergence era.

**What's in it:**
- PostgreSQL database with cleaned schema and sector normalisation
- Four SQL analysis files (schema, load, exploration, findings)
- Two-page Power BI dashboard with custom DAX measures and page navigation
- 12-page polished findings document with executive summary, methodology, and caveats

**Highlighted findings:**
- Composite ranking framework combining growth, profitability, and scale
- Three decline patterns identified (death spiral, structural shrinkage, strategic disposal)
- Twin NPM peaks (2011 and 2021) with structurally different sector composition
- Electronics divergence: INTC 4.6% CAGR vs NVDA 17.2% CAGR — same sector, opposite outcomes

**Tools:** PostgreSQL · Power BI · DAX
