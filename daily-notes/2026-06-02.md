## Key concepts
- JOIN = stitching two tables together using a matching rule (ON clause)
- INNER JOIN: only matches | LEFT JOIN: keep everything left, match where possible
- Aliases keep multi-table queries readable
- The pattern: FROM table1 AS t1 JOIN table2 AS t2 ON t1.x = t2.x
