# 🎮 Global Video Game Sales Analysis (1980–2020)

A complete data‑analysis case study exploring 40 years of global video game sales.  
This project demonstrates strong analytical thinking, clean code, and professional storytelling — ideal for showcasing data‑analysis skills on GitHub.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This analysis examines physical video game sales from 1980 to 2020 across four major regions:

- North America  
- Europe  
- Japan  
- Other regions  

The goal is to uncover long‑term industry trends, regional preferences, genre lifecycles, and publisher dominance.

---

## 🔍 Key Questions Answered

This notebook answers six core analytical questions:

1. **Who are the top publishers globally?**  
2. **How have global sales changed over time?**  
3. **How has genre popularity evolved?**  
4. **How do genre preferences differ by region?**  
5. **Which publishers dominate each region?**  
6. **How much do the top 10 games contribute to total sales?**

Each section includes clean code, polished visualizations, and clear interpretation.

---

## 🧠 Summary of Findings

### 🏆 Publisher Dominance
- **Nintendo** is the global leader due to evergreen IP and platform exclusivity.  
- **EA** dominates Europe (FIFA).  
- **Activision** dominates North America (Call of Duty).  
- **Square Enix, Capcom, Konami** dominate Japan (JRPGs, Monster Hunter).

### 📈 Sales Trends Over Time
- Sales peaked during the **PS2 / DS / Wii / PS3 / Xbox 360** era (2001–2008).  
- Post‑2010 decline reflects **shift to digital**, not industry collapse.  
- Post‑2015 drop is due to **dataset limitations**.

### 🎭 Genre Popularity
- **Action, Sports, Shooter** dominate NA/EU.  
- **Role‑Playing and Platform** dominate Japan.  
- Genre trends align perfectly with regional publisher success.

### 🎯 Market Concentration
- The **top 10 games contribute only ~4%** of total global sales.  
- Gaming is **franchise‑driven**, not hit‑driven.  
- Thousands of mid‑tier games contribute meaningfully to total sales.
